# Frame — Vibe From Cafe

## Canvas
- 1920x1080 @ 30fps. Safe margin 110px. 22.0s total, four scenes.

## Palette
- Ground: black (#000000) / surface (#111111)
- Text: white (#FFFFFF), muted (#999999)
- Accent: yellow (#F5C400) for emphasis, the rule, and the progress line
- Logo: black+yellow artwork sits on a white plate (never knocked out on dark)

## Type
- Display: Inter 800, tracking -3%
- Body: Inter 400/500/600, tracked labels at .30-.34em
- Hero/title 130px · scene lines 84px · l1 104px · stat 220px · support 34-38px · footer 22px

## Motion personality
- Confident, energy 0.65. Entrances `power3.out`, exits `power2.in`, drift `sine.inOut`.
- Hard cuts between scenes; each scene enters strong and eases into a hold.
- A single yellow progress line runs the full 22s for continuity.

## Signature devices
- White "menu card" plate holding the logo.
- Yellow tracked kicker above every hero.
- Yellow accent word inside each message line.

## Do not
- Do not knock the logo out onto black (its black elements disappear).
- Do not animate layout; use transforms and opacity only.
